## Who I Am

I am a self-taught software developer from Murang'a, Kenya, currently pursuing a **Bachelor of Public Administration & Governance** at Murang'a University of Technology (graduating 2027). I started coding in 2022 out of curiosity and have since built and deployed **31+ applications** spanning web, mobile, and artificial intelligence — entirely on my own.

My journey began with C++, evolved through the full web stack (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Django, PostgreSQL), and has expanded into **AI/ML engineering** and **Android development**. In June 2026 I released **Kenji Assistant**, an AI-powered voice assistant for Android that I built from scratch over six months.

Alongside technology, I have a grounding in **public policy, governance, and community development** — demonstrated through applied research for Narok County and multi-stakeholder community initiatives in Murang'a County. I believe technology and good governance are not separate — they are complementary tools for building better public services.
